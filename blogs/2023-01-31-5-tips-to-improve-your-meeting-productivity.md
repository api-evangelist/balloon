A lot has changed in the last few years, and smooth transitions require significant back-and-forth—and usually that means a lot of meetings. These days, remote workers, which make up around two-thirds of the American workforce, are spending 29% more time in meetings, and the adverse effects like increased screen time, Zoom fatigue, and diminished productivity take a toll.
